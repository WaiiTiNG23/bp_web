# BP-Dev Design System

## Direction

Digital precision studio. The site should feel like a capable product team presenting a working system: editorial scale, technical alignment, restrained blue signals and real interface evidence. It avoids neon gradients, generic developer imagery and repeated equal cards.

## Mode

Persuade. The visitor must understand what BP-Dev solves, see one honest proof of execution and reach a conversation without friction.

## Visual language

- Near-black ink surfaces with cool slate elevation and electric blue as the only accent.
- Display typography: Manrope. Technical labels and metadata: IBM Plex Mono.
- Radius system: 12px controls, 20px content surfaces, 28px feature frames.
- Fine grid lines, hairline borders and cropped interface imagery provide structure.
- Icons use Phosphor regular/duotone only.
- No decorative pills, generic stock imagery, fake metrics or invented proof.

## Motion

- Intensity 5/10: purposeful and quiet.
- Scroll reveals use opacity and an 18px translate, once per element.
- Buttons compress to 0.98 on press; feature links shift their arrow by 3px.
- Hover motion is restricted to fine pointers.
- Reduced motion removes transforms and reveal delays.

## Responsive

- Desktop uses asymmetric layouts within one continuous landing-page narrative.
- Tablet collapses feature compositions before typography becomes cramped.
- Mobile keeps one strong reading column, 44px minimum targets and no horizontal overflow.

## Content rules

- Lead with outcomes in plain Spanish.
- Keep technical detail available but secondary.
- Plataforma B2B is the only project claim until more evidence exists.
- One primary action per section; contact and agenda are distinct intents.
